About Clearfold

Clearfold is an independent, on-device RSS news aggregator for Android. Clearfold does not publish original journalism or editorialize news articles. Instead, it connects directly from the user's device to publicly available RSS/Atom feeds from 164+ global newsrooms, groups related headlines, displays open media-spectrum context, and opens full articles directly on each original publisher's website.

Attribution & Editorial Independence

Clearfold is independently developed and is not affiliated with, funded by, or endorsed by any political party, government, or news organization. Every headline and summary snippet includes explicit source attribution and a direct link to the original publisher. Media bias and factuality labels are derived from the open-source News Media Bias & Factuality Dataset (Burdisso et al., Idiap Research Institute / MBFC, Apache-2.0 License).

Publisher Opt-Out & Corrections

News publishers who wish to update their RSS feed URL, correct attribution metadata, or opt out of appearing in Clearfold can email support.clearnews@gmail.com. Verified requests are processed promptly.
