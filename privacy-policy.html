Privacy Policy

App: ClearNews (org.clearfold.news) • Effective Date: November 2026

ClearNews is built from the ground up as a privacy-first, on-device RSS news reader. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

ClearNews does not require or offer account registration, logins, or user profiles. The app contains zero advertising SDKs, third-party analytics trackers, telemetry SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.

2. 100% On-Device Storage & Processing

All of your settings and reading data stay strictly on your Android device (stored locally via Android Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore), including:

  • Selected news regions, followed topics, and reading lens preferences
  • Favorite channels, muted channels, custom RSS feeds, and blocked keywords
  • Bookmarked stories and cached RSS headlines for fast offline viewing

Multi-source story clustering, political spectrum comparisons, and extractive Pulse Summaries are computed entirely locally on your device. You can clear cached images or rebuild the local feed cache at any time from Settings → Local Cache & Fast Loading, or remove all data by uninstalling the app.

3. Direct Network Requests to News Publishers

ClearNews connects directly from your device to public RSS and Atom feeds provided by news publishers (and any custom RSS feeds you add) to retrieve headlines, summaries, and images. When you open a full article, ClearNews routes you directly to the publisher's website via Chrome Custom Tabs. ClearNews does not operate a tracking proxy or collect your browsing history.

4. No Location Tracking & Minimal Permissions

ClearNews never requests GPS, location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions. News regions are chosen manually by you inside the app. Optional notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) is used solely to deliver local, on-device breaking news alerts when you are not actively using the app.

5. Publisher Attribution, Non-Affiliation & Opt-Out

ClearNews is an independent RSS reader and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any news publisher listed in the app. All publisher trademarks, headlines, and articles belong to their respective owners. Channel bias and factuality metadata are matched against the open-source News Media Bias & Factuality Dataset (Idiap Research Institute / MBFC, Apache-2.0 License).

6. Children's Privacy

ClearNews is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
